Travel and local transport

The San Francesco Complex is inside the historic centre, approximately a 20-minute walk from Lucca railway station.

Routes and journey times are indicative. Please consult the transport operators for timetables and fares before travelling.

By plane

Pisa International Airport “Galileo Galilei” (PSA)

Pisa is the closest airport to Lucca, approximately 30 km away. The journey by shuttle and regional train usually takes 45–60 minutes, excluding waiting time:

  1. Take the PisaMover shuttle from the terminal to Pisa Centrale railway station.
  2. Take a Trenitalia regional train from Pisa Centrale to Lucca. The rail journey takes approximately 25–30 minutes.
  3. Continue to the venue on foot (see By train) or by taxi.

Alternative: Autolinee Toscane operates services between Pisa Airport and Lucca (Piazzale Verdi). Consult Autolinee Toscane for the current timetable.

Florence Airport “Amerigo Vespucci” (FLR)

Florence Airport is approximately 70 km from Lucca. There are two public-transport routes, both starting on tram line T2 at the terminal:

  • Tram and train: T2 to Firenze Santa Maria Novella, then a regional train to Lucca (approximately 1 hour 20 minutes on direct services). Around 2 hours in total.
  • Tram and coach: T2 to the Guidoni stop, a few minutes from the airport, then the Autolinee Toscane Firenze–Lucca coach (line DD) from the Guidoni interchange to Lucca Piazzale Verdi, approximately 1 hour. There is no need to continue to Santa Maria Novella. The coach does not run on Sundays and last departures are in the early evening.

By train

Lucca railway station (Piazzale Ricasoli) is just outside the southern city walls and approximately 15–20 minutes on foot from the San Francesco Complex.

Direct regional trains connect Lucca with Pisa, Viareggio, Montecatini Terme, Pistoia, and Florence. Timetables, fares, and tickets are available from Trenitalia.

By bus / coach

Regional bus services are operated by Autolinee Toscane. Lucca's main bus terminal is at Piazzale Verdi, approximately a 20-minute walk from the venue. Consult the Lucca route and timetable information before travelling.

  • Firenze–Lucca (line DD): from the Firenze T2 Guidoni interchange to Piazzale Verdi, approximately 1 hour. No service on Sundays.
  • Pisa–Lucca: regional services between the two cities and from Pisa Airport (see By plane).

By car

Lucca's historic centre is largely pedestrianised and covered, for most of the area inside the walls, by a camera-enforced Zona a Traffico Limitato (ZTL). Public car parks such as Parcheggio Mazzini and Parcheggio Palatucci can be reached without crossing the ZTL.

Participants staying inside the walls should contact their accommodation provider about vehicle access: properties can register a guest's number plate for arrival and departure.

From the A11 motorway, take the Lucca Est exit for the venue side of town. Convenient car parks:

  • Parcheggio Mazzini (underground; Via dei Bacchettoni, entered from Porta Elisa) — the closest car park to the San Francesco Complex.
  • Parcheggio Carducci (Porta San Pietro, near the railway station).
  • Parcheggio Palatucci (Porta Santa Maria, on the northern side) — the largest.

Taxis

  • Radiotaxi Lucca: +39 0583 1745 — 24 hours, also bookable through the appTaxi app.
  • Lucca Taxi: +39 0583 1646464 · lucca.taxi

Taxi ranks and further operators are listed by the Lucca tourist board. Passengers who require a wheelchair-accessible vehicle should contact an operator in advance to confirm availability.

Getting around Lucca

The venue, railway station, Piazzale Verdi bus terminal, and many accommodation options are within walking distance of one another. Bicycle hire is available at several locations in and around the historic centre.

Practical information

  • Currency: Euro (EUR); credit/debit cards are widely accepted.
  • Electricity: 230 V, plug types F and L (bring an adapter if needed).
  • Emergency number: 112 (EU-wide).
  • Pharmacies: several in the historic centre (look for the green cross; rotating night service posted on pharmacy doors).
  • Dining hours: restaurants typically serve lunch from 12:30 to 14:30 and dinner from 19:30.
  • Weather: early September is generally warm, but rain is possible; check the forecast before travelling.
