Code of Conduct

CONFEST 2027 is committed to a professional, inclusive, and harassment-free environment. This Code of Conduct applies to all conference activities, including workshops, social events, and official online channels.

By registering for CONFEST 2027 you agree to abide by this Code of Conduct.

Expected behavior

  • Be respectful and considerate in speech and actions, in person and online.
  • Engage in scientific debate constructively: critique ideas, not people.
  • Be welcoming toward newcomers and colleagues from all backgrounds and career stages.
  • Follow the rules of the conference venue and social-event locations, and respect their staff.
  • Report conduct that may violate this code or place a participant at risk.

Unacceptable behavior

Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Offensive, demeaning, or discriminatory comments or behavior related to gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, national origin, age, religion, or career stage.
  • Sexual language or imagery in talks, materials, or public spaces; unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
  • Intimidation, stalking, or following; sustained disruption of talks or other events.
  • Inappropriate physical contact or invasion of personal space.
  • Harassing photography or recording; publishing private communications without consent.
  • Personal attacks, insults, or trolling, including in online channels and on social media in connection with the conference.
  • Advocating for, or encouraging, any of the above.

Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation of this Code of Conduct, please contact the person listed below. Reports will be handled discreetly and information will be shared only as necessary to assess the report and protect participants.

  • Mirco Tribastone (IMT School for Advanced Studies Lucca, Italy) - General Chair

During the conference, reports may also be made in person to a member of the organizing team or at the registration desk.

Accessibility and inclusion

To discuss specific accessibility requirements, please contact the organizers as early as possible at confest2027@imtlucca.it.
