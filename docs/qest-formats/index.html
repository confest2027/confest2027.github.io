About QEST+FORMATS

QEST+FORMATS brings together the communities of the International Conference on Quantitative Evaluation of Systems (QEST) and the International Conference on Formal Modeling and Analysis of Timed Systems (FORMATS). The conference covers quantitative methods for evaluating and verifying computer systems and networks, as well as the modelling, design, and analysis of timed systems. Further information about the series is available at qest.org.
