About QEST+FORMATS

QEST+FORMATS brings together the communities of the International Conference on Quantitative Evaluation of Systems (QEST) and the International Conference on Formal Modeling and Analysis of Timed Systems (FORMATS). The conference covers quantitative methods for evaluating and verifying computer systems and networks, as well as the modelling, design, and analysis of timed systems. Further information about the series is available at qest.org.

Call for papers

QEST+FORMATS 2027 invites submissions on quantitative evaluation and the modelling and analysis of timed systems. Topics include:

  • Quantitative modeling formalisms such as Markov chains and Markov decision processes, stochastic Petri nets, queueing models, stochastic process algebras, stochastic games, and probabilistic programs;
  • Timed systems: timed automata and their extensions, semantics and expressiveness of timed models, real-time scheduling, clock synchronization, and worst-case execution time analysis;
  • Hybrid systems and cyber-physical systems: modeling, verification, and control;
  • Analysis techniques: probabilistic and statistical model checking, quantitative verification and synthesis, parameter estimation and inference, simulation, abstraction, and compositional methods;
  • Performance, dependability, reliability, safety, and security evaluation of computer systems and networks;
  • Quantitative analysis of machine-learning components and learning-based methods for quantitative modeling and verification;
  • Tools, case studies, and industrial applications of quantitative and timed-systems methods, including artifacts supporting reproducible evaluation.
