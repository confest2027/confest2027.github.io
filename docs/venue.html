Venue

CONFEST 2027 will be held at the San Francesco Complex of the IMT School for Advanced Studies Lucca. The restored former monastic complex is in the eastern part of Lucca's historic centre and houses lecture rooms, a library, and conference facilities.

Address:
IMT School for Advanced Studies Lucca
San Francesco Complex

55100 Lucca (LU), Italy

Open in Google Maps IMT: how to reach us

On foot from the station: the campus is approximately 1.2 km from Lucca railway station, a walk of 15–20 minutes. Enter the historic centre near Porta San Gervasio or Porta Elisa and continue towards Piazza San Francesco. The Piazzale Verdi bus terminal is on the western side of the centre, about 20 minutes away on foot.

Getting there

Directions from Pisa and Florence airports, together with rail, bus, and driving information, are available on the Travel page.

Arriving by car

The nearest motorway exit is A11 — Lucca Est. The closest car park to the venue is the Parcheggio Mazzini underground car park (Via dei Bacchettoni, entered from Porta Elisa), adjacent to the campus. Most of the area inside the walls is a camera-enforced ZTL: see the driving and parking notes.

About Lucca

Lucca is known for its well-preserved Renaissance walls, which form a tree-lined circuit around the historic centre. Notable sites include Piazza dell'Anfiteatro, the Romanesque churches of the old city, Torre Guinigi, and the birthplace of composer Giacomo Puccini.
