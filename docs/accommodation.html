Accommodation

Early booking is recommended. September is high season in Lucca, and accommodation in the historic centre consists mainly of small hotels, guesthouses, and bed-and-breakfasts.

When searching independently, use “centro storico” for properties in the historic centre.

Areas

  • Inside the walls

    Closest to the venue

    Many properties are within a short walk of the venue. Properties inside the walls can usually arrange ZTL access for arrival and departure; ask when booking (see the driving information).

  • Just outside the walls

    Near the station and main roads

    Several larger hotels are outside the walls, including near the railway station. These generally offer easier road access and are approximately 15–25 minutes from the venue on foot.

  • Apartments & B&Bs

    Suitable for groups and longer stays

    Rental apartments and family-run bed-and-breakfasts are available both inside and outside the walls.
