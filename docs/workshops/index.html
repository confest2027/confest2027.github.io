Call for Workshop Proposals

CONFEST 2027 invites proposals for affiliated workshops on topics related to concurrency theory, formal methods, quantitative evaluation, and timed and probabilistic systems. Proposals for established series and new workshops are both welcome.

Proposal format

Proposals should be 1–2 pages and include:

  • Workshop name and acronym
  • Organizers, listed as Name (Affiliation, Country) - Organizer, and a primary contact
  • A concise description of the scope, topics, and relevance to CONFEST
  • History of the workshop series, if any (previous editions and attendance)
  • Expected number of participants
  • Proposed format, including invited talks, contributed papers, or discussion sessions, and whether a half or full day is requested
  • Plans for proceedings, if applicable
