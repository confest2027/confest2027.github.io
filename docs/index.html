CONFEST 2027 brings together researchers, practitioners, and students working on concurrency theory, stochastic and timed systems, and formal methods.

The programme comprises CONCUR, QEST+FORMATS, AnnaLucaFest, and affiliated workshops.

4–11 September 2027

San Francesco Complex · Lucca, Italy
